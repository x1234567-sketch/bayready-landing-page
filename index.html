Built for small independent Irish garages

Know which cars are ready, without chasing paper notes.

BayReady is a prototype garage job tracker designed for independent repair businesses with 3–6 mechanics and one office administrator.

Try the working demo

Student prototype using fictional jobs. Not a live commercial service.

Less confusion between the workshop and the office

Workshop updates

Mechanics tap a button to move a job through five repair stages, without typing.

Customer lookup

A customer can look up a fictional sample job using a job number or registration.

Office overview

See jobs grouped by stage, overdue parts warnings, and how many cars are ready to collect.

The problem we're exploring

In a busy small garage, staff may spend time checking the workshop, answering calls about repair progress, and remembering which cars are waiting for parts. BayReady explores whether a simple status tracker could make that information easier to find.
